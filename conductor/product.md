# Product Definition: PulseRival

## 🚀 Vision
PulseRival is a high-performance health gamification platform that transforms fitness data into a high-stakes, social competition. It moves beyond passive tracking to create "Big Win" moments through its unique **Velocity Vault** engine.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Social Competitors:** Friends and families who want to challenge each other in high-energy "Speed Duels."
- **Goal-Seekers:** Casual fitness enthusiasts looking for probabilistic rewards and community-driven motivation.
- **Global Challengers:** Users who want to participate in massive "Global Vault" events for significant payouts.

## 💎 Core Mechanic: The Velocity Vault
The system uses "Speed Weighting" to reward intensity and promptness.
- **The Pool:** Every participant contributes to a dynamic "Vault."
- **Weighting:** The sooner a user hits the challenge goal (steps, calories, distance), the higher their "Entry Weight" (Probability Tier).
    - **Elite Tier (<48hrs):** 10x Weight.
    - **Pro Tier (48–96hrs):** 5x Weight.
    - **Finisher Tier (<7 days):** 1x Weight.
- **The Burst:** At the end of the window, the Vault "bursts," distributing massive rewards to weighted winners and a "Finisher's Dividend" to everyone else.

## 🛠 Key Features
- **Public Challenges (The Global Vault):** Massive events with probability brackets and viral "Big Win" payouts.
- **Private Challenges (The Friend Duel):** Customizable group races with a 24-hour "Speed Bonus" clock triggered by the first finisher.
- **Social Engagement:** "Nudge" and "Taunt" notifications, challenge discovery feeds, and a trophy room for permanent glory.
- **Anti-Dilution logic:** Ensuring rewards remain significant even as participation grows.
